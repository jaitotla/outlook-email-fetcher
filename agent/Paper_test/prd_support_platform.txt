AI-Powered Customer Support Platform – Product Requirements Document

Product Overview
================
An AI-powered customer support platform that automates ticket routing, provides intelligent responses, and escalates complex issues to humans. The platform reduces support cost by 40% while improving customer satisfaction. Target users are SaaS companies and online retailers with 1000+ customers.

Customer Support Challenge
==========================
Companies struggle with ticket volume, inconsistent response quality, and long resolution times. Support teams spend time on repetitive questions that could be automated. Complex issues get delayed waiting for human agents. Customers expect 24/7 support across multiple channels. Scaling support teams linearly with revenue is expensive.

Product Goals
=============
Reduce average response time from 24 hours to under 1 hour. Achieve 80% of tickets resolved without human intervention. Maintain CSAT score above 90% through consistent quality. Reduce support cost per ticket by 50%. Enable support teams to focus on complex, high-value issues.

Core Features
=============
Intelligent Ticket Routing uses ML to classify tickets and assign to appropriate queue. Self-Service Portal surfaces FAQ, knowledge base articles, and community answers. AI Chatbot answers common questions with understanding of product context. Ticket Triage prioritizes critical issues and detects sentiment. Human Escalation flags tickets requiring human expertise. Multi-Channel Support integrates email, chat, social media, and phone.

AI Chatbot Capabilities
=======================
Natural language understanding recognizes user intent from varied phrasings. Product Knowledge Graph stores FAQ, documentation, and solution guides. Contextual awareness uses conversation history and customer data. Confidence scoring prevents low-confidence responses. Human Handoff seamlessly connects to agents when needed. Learning from corrections improves accuracy over time.

Ticket Management
=================
Central inbox consolidates tickets from all channels. Custom workflows route tickets based on rules and ML classification. SLA tracking ensures timely responses and escalation. Collaboration tools enable agents to work together on complex issues. Ticket templates standardize responses for common problems. Time tracking for billing and metrics.

Knowledge Management
====================
Knowledge base organization with categories and tags. Article versioning tracks changes and maintains history. Analytics show which articles help resolve issues. Integration with support chat embeds relevant articles. SEO optimization for public-facing articles. Multi-language support for global teams.

Analytics and Reporting
=======================
Real-time dashboards show team performance and ticket metrics. CSAT trends and sentiment analysis. Response time and resolution time tracking. Cost per ticket and team utilization metrics. AI performance metrics and automation rate. Automated reports sent daily to managers.

Agent Tools
===========
Agent desktop with ticket details, knowledge base, and chat history. Quick reply templates for common responses. Ticket notes and internal commentary. Customer history and purchase records. Canned responses with one-click insertion. Real-time suggestions for agent responses.

Customer Portal
===============
Self-service ticket creation and tracking for customers. Community forum for peer-to-peer support. Knowledge base search and article recommendations. Status page showing system health. Chat widget enables direct communication. Account settings and preferences.

Integrations
============
CRM integration with Salesforce to access customer data. Help desk integration with Zendesk and Freshdesk migration. E-commerce integration with Shopify and WooCommerce. Communication integrations with Slack and Teams. Analytics integration with Google Analytics.

Technical Requirements
======================
API-first architecture enables easy integration. Webhook notifications for real-time updates. Batch APIs for bulk operations. Rate limiting prevents abuse. Authentication via API keys and OAuth 2.0. Comprehensive API documentation and SDKs.

Security and Compliance
=======================
End-to-end encryption for ticket content. GDPR compliance with data deletion and portability. HIPAA compliance for healthcare customers. SOC 2 Type II certification. Regular penetration testing. Audit logs for all sensitive operations.

Pricing Model
==============
Per-agent pricing starting at $99/agent/month. Volume discounts for 10+ agents. Feature tiers: Basic, Professional, Enterprise. Overage charges for tickets beyond plan limits. Annual billing discounts of 20%. Free tier for startups with up to 3 agents.

Success Metrics
===============
Achieve 70% ticket deflection rate through AI. Support NPS above 50. Agent satisfaction above 4/5. Platform reliability 99.9% uptime. Customer onboarding completes in under 2 hours. 80% of customers renew annually.

Implementation Timeline
=======================
Phase 1 (Months 1-3): MVP with ticket system and basic chatbot. Phase 2 (Months 4-6): Advanced AI routing and knowledge base. Phase 3 (Months 7-9): Integrations and multi-channel support. Phase 4 (Months 10-12): Advanced analytics and ML improvements.

Competitive Positioning
=======================
Price 30% lower than Zendesk and Intercom. Faster time-to-value with 1-week implementation. Superior AI using latest NLP models. Industry-specific templates reduce configuration. Better agent experience with minimal training required.

Future Roadmap
==============
Predictive analytics to forecast volume and staff needs. Proactive outreach based on customer behavior. Video support with AI-powered video analysis. Conversational commerce seamlessly connect support to sales. Advanced sentiment analysis for emotional insights.
