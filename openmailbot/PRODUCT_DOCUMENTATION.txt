WHAT IS OPENMAILBOT?
OpenMailBot is an intelligent email assistant that reads your emails and 
attachments, then answers your questions about them using AI.

WHAT CAN IT DO?
• Search email threads and attachments intelligently
• Answer questions about your email conversations
• Read PDFs, spreadsheets, and presentations
• Find information without manual scrolling

HOW TO USE IT
1. Prepare your emails and group them into threads
2. Start processing - the system analyzes content automatically
3. Ask questions like: "What was decided?" or "Where's the budget?"
4. Get answers backed by actual email content

SUPPORTED FILES
• PDF documents
• Excel/CSV spreadsheets
• PowerPoint presentations
• Email messages

COMMON QUESTIONS

Q: How is my data stored?
A: Locally and securely. Each user has isolated data storage with their own 
   copies of emails and attachments.

Q: How fast is processing?
A: Initial processing takes time based on email volume. Searching is fast 
   afterward. Large threads may take several minutes.

Q: What files can it read?
A: PDFs, spreadsheets, presentations, and email text content.

Q: Is it accurate?
A: Yes, but verify important decisions in the original emails.

BEST PRACTICES
• Ask clear, specific questions
• Include context in your queries
• Process emails regularly, not in large batches
• Keep related emails in the same thread

TROUBLESHOOTING
• "Thread not found" → Check thread data is saved
• "No information found" → Rephrase your question
• "Processing slow" → This is normal for large files
• "Embedding error" → Check your internet connection

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
• 2GB storage space
• Stable internet connection
• Modern web browser
• Email account with accessible data

KEY BENEFITS
✓ Save time finding information
✓ Understand long conversations easily
✓ Search multiple documents at once
✓ Private, organized data storage
✓ Accurate AI-powered answers
